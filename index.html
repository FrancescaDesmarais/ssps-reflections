About this project

The project

This is a small, personal project built by Francesca Desmarais. Francesca has been building participatory experiences for people to explore the Shared Socioeconomic Pathways and use these scenarios in creative, foresight projects. Between a pandemic, developments in AI, wars, and record heat—there's been a lot going on the past few years. In the vein of participation, this quiz is a small sense-check on how people are interpreting the world events happening, through the long-term lens of the climate crisis.

It will stay open until a critical mass of respondents. Please share.

The SSPs

The Shared Socioeconomic Pathways are five scenarios that cover five different development pathways global society could take towards 2100. They are used as inputs to global climate models and as part of the IPCC assessments.
