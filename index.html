About the SSPs

The Shared Socioeconomic Pathways (SSPs) are five scenarios describing possible development pathways global society could take toward 2100. They explore how broad changes in population, education, economic growth, inequality, governance, technology, and energy and land use create or reduce our ability to mitigate and adapt to climate change. The SSPs are used as inputs to global climate models and form part of the IPCC assessments — the closest thing we have to a shared map of where humanity might be heading.
